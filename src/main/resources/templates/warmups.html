Warm-up Exercises

Perform warm-up exercises in order before playing sports to avoid injuries !
Duration: 30 seconds per exercise | Rest: None

1. March

Stand straight and start lifting your knees slightly off the ground and swinging your arms with your knee movements to maintain balance.

30 seconds
2. Stretch Jacks

Stand straight then reach your right arm to the opposite side while simultaneously lifting your right knee after that repeat the movement by reach your left arm to the opposite side while simultaneously lifting your left knee.

30 seconds
3. Marching Step

Raise your right arm upwards and lift your left knee at the same time then return to the start and repeat on the other side by lifting your left arm and right knee together.

30 seconds
4. Standing Toe Touches

Stand with your legs apart and keep them as straight as possible. Slowly bend forward and reach your arms down to touch your toes then repeat the movement on the other side.

30 seconds
5. Arm Circles

Plant your feet firmly on the ground then extend your arms out to the sides and start moving them in slow, circular motions.

30 seconds
6. Lunges

Get into a lunge position with your back knee very close to touching the ground and your hands clasped in front of your chest then push your body back up using your front foot and the other leg.

30 seconds
7. Lateral Lunges

Take a wide step to the side and lower your weight onto it as if you are doing a squat while keeping your other leg slightly bent, clasp your hands in front of you then push yourself back to the center and repeat on the other side.

30 seconds
8. Cross Jacks

Start in a natural standing position with your arms raised in front of you then do a light jump while spreading your arms and legs outwards so your body forms a starfish shape then quickly return to the starting position.

30 seconds
